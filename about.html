Our Story

A family's recipe, written across two decades.

Bodega Bistro, 2003 – 2017.

For fourteen years, Matthew Ho's family ran Bodega Bistro in San Francisco's Tenderloin District, serving traditional Northern Vietnamese cuisine to neighbors, regulars, and the city at large.

When the restaurant closed in 2017, the Ho family's cooking didn't stop. The brand continued through pop-up events and meal packages during the pandemic — keeping the family-style spirit of the kitchen alive while the next chapter took shape.

Bodega SF, est. 2022.

In 2022, Bodega SF opened its brick-and-mortar location in the Tenderloin District — bringing high-quality Vietnamese cuisine back to the community through a modernized, casual fine-dining experience.

The restaurant centers on a family-style concept of authentic and creative dishes — a direct continuation of the Ho family's table, now set for a new generation of guests.
