Reservation Information

What to know before you book.

Booking Window

Reservations open 30 days in advance through OpenTable.

Party Size

OpenTable reservations are available for parties up to 8. For parties of 9 or more, contact the restaurant by email at info@bodegarestaurants.com.

Ask Billy Tasting Menu

Dinner reservations of 10 or more are required to participate in the Ask Billy tasting menu. $90 per person.

Dietary Restrictions

Please indicate dietary restrictions when making your reservation. Same-day dietary requests may not be accommodated.

Credit Card & Cancellations

A credit card is required for reservations. The cancellation / change fee is $25 per guest when a cancellation or change occurs within two hours of the reservation.

Table Hold

Tables are held for 15 minutes after the reservation time.

Children

Children are welcome. Children may not sit at the bar counter. Infants must be included in the reservation headcount.

Walk-Ins

Walk-ins are welcome as availability allows. Guests may join the waitlist in person.

Seating

Reservations are available for the dining room and high-top tables. The restaurant offers indoor seating only.

Reservation Policies

Table duration & late arrivals.

Please review the official Bodega SF policies on dining time and late or incomplete parties.

Table duration
  • Parties of 2: up to 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • Parties of 3 – 5: up to 2 hours.
  • Parties of 6 or more: up to 2 hours 15 minutes for dinner.
Running late?

Guests who are running late should message the restaurant through OpenTable. The table will continue to be held for the reservation; however, the dining time remains based on the original reservation time.

Incomplete parties

Incomplete parties can still be seated. The reserved dining window continues from the original reservation time.

Dietary restrictions

Bodega SF offers gluten-free and vegetarian items. Please mention any dietary restrictions when making your reservation. The restaurant will guide guests through the available options. Please note that some allergies or dietary restrictions may not be accommodated due to the nature of Vietnamese cuisine. Same-day dietary requests may not be accommodated.

Credit card, cancellation & change fees

A credit card is required to secure a reservation. The cancellation / change fee is $25 per guest when a cancellation or change occurs within two hours of the reservation.
