Guest Services

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your visit. Policies and information are listed exactly as provided by Bodega SF.

Where is Bodega SF located?

Bodega SF is located in the Tenderloin District near Union Square at 138 Mason St., next to the Found Hotel.

How far in advance do reservations open?

30 days in advance.

Do you take large parties?

OpenTable reservations are available for parties up to 8. For larger groups, contact info@bodegarestaurants.com.

Takeout

Lunch and dinner takeout menus are different from dine-in menus. Lunch takeout is available during lunch. Dinner takeout is available during dinner.

Do you do private events?

Yes. See private events information →

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, as availability allows. Guests may join the waitlist in person.

Do you offer outdoor seating?

No. The restaurant offers indoor seating only. Reservations are available for dining room and high-top tables.

Corkage

$30 per bottle, 750ml or equivalent. Maximum 2 bottles per reservation that are not already listed on the wine list.

Can I bring my own dessert?

Yes. Cake / dessert fee: $4 per person.

Gift cards

Physical gift cards can be purchased in person. Digital gift cards are available online through ToastTab.

Dog policy

Non-ADA-certified animals are not allowed inside the restaurant. Service animals are permitted.

Parking

Garage: 233 Ellis St.

Parking with Bodega's stamp:

  • $15 Sunday – Thursday
  • $20 Friday / Saturday
  • Cash only on weekends.

Street parking is also available.

Public transportation

Closest BART / Muni station: Powell Station. Approximately a 5-minute walk.

ADA access

ADA access and entry is available through the Found Hotel next door.

Dietary restrictions

Bodega SF offers gluten-free and vegetarian items. Guests should mention dietary restrictions when making reservations. The restaurant will guide guests through available options. Some allergies / dietary restrictions may not be accommodated due to the nature of Vietnamese cuisine.

Catering

Bodega SF accepts catering inquiries. Delivery and pickup are available in San Francisco. Orders must be placed 3 days in advance. Contact info@bodegarestaurants.com.

Table duration
  • Parties of 2: up to 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • Parties of 3 – 5: up to 2 hours.
  • Parties of 6 or more: up to 2 hours 15 minutes for dinner.
Late or incomplete parties
  • Guests who are running late should message the restaurant through OpenTable.
  • The table will continue to be held for the reservation.
  • However, the dining time remains based on the original reservation time.
  • Incomplete parties can still be seated, but the reserved dining window continues from the original reservation time.
