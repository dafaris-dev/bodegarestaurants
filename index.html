◆ Our Story

Two decades, one family's table.

Bodega Bistro Opens

Matthew Ho's family opens Bodega Bistro in San Francisco's Tenderloin District, serving traditional Northern Vietnamese cuisine.

2003
2017

Bodega Bistro Closes

After fourteen years in the Tenderloin, the original restaurant closes. The Ho family's cooking continues.

Pop-ups & Meal Packages

Through the pandemic, the Bodega brand continues through pop-up events and meal packages, keeping the family-style spirit alive.

Bodega SF Opens
