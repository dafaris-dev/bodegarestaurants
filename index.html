Our Story

From a family bistro to a modern table.

Bodega SF is an evolution of Matthew Ho's family's restaurant, Bodega Bistro — which operated from 2003 to 2017 in San Francisco's Tenderloin District, serving traditional Northern Vietnamese cuisine. Through pop-up events and meal packages during the pandemic, the brand continued, and in 2022 Bodega SF opened its brick-and-mortar home.

Today, Bodega SF brings high-quality Vietnamese cuisine back to the community through a modernized, casual fine-dining experience built around a family-style concept of authentic and creative dishes.

Reservations

A seat at the table.

Reservations open 30 days in advance via OpenTable for parties up to 8. For parties of 9 or more, please contact the restaurant by email. Dinner reservations of 10 or more are required to participate in the Ask Billy tasting menu.
